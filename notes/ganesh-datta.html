Ganesh datta

People ask me ganesh datta like it is a trick. It is not. If booking, follow-up, and CRM ownership are not installed, you are the system, and the system fails the minute you get tired.

The actual answer

The public writeups on this are usually written by people who have never sat in the calendar.

I start with the path a lead takes after they say yes to a call. If that path lives in your head, we are not talking about marketing. We are talking about memory. Memory does not scale.

What I install first

Call-booking goes live in the first day when I can. Reminders, no-show recovery, and a follow-up sequence that does not wait for you to feel professional. CRM ownership has to be visible. If two people can both assume the other sent the note, nobody sent it.

Content only matters after that. If the content engine depends on mood, it is not a system. I tie posts and clips to the pipeline you already run, not to a content calendar that looks busy and sells nothing.

Where operators fight me

They want a prettier funnel before the ugly one works. I do not. Days 1 to 7 are booking and follow-up. Week 2 hardens CRM and admin. Inside 30 days the environment should stop needing heroics.

I guarantee delivery and execution. I do not guarantee your close rate. Anyone who promises revenue without touching how you follow up is selling a story.

A check you can run tonight

Open last week's inbound. Count how many people waited more than a day. That number is the leak. Tools will not fix it until someone owns the next step in writing.
