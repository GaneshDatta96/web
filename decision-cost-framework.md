# The Decision Cost Framework

The Decision Cost Framework is Ganesh Datta's original method for a narrow question: what does it cost to keep deciding this by hand?

Decision Cost is the organizational cost of using human judgment to start, approve, prioritize, interpret, escalate, or authorize work when Automate, Assist, or Keep Human would be a better split. It is not all labor. The unit is a Decision Episode.

Decision Cost Audit is the engagement: one workflow lane, a human interview, an AI draft, a human lock, one measured quick win.

v0.1. Declared 10 August 2026. Code 100705. Not certified. Adjacent research (transaction cost, cognitive load, automation) is cited. Nobody accredited this method.

## Public scores

- Load — person-minutes per week spent deciding, retrieving, coordinating
- Exposure — how bad the current mode is, 1 to 5
- Disposition — Automate, Assist, or Keep Human. Forced.

No single mega-multiplicative vanity score.

## Automate / Assist / Keep Human

- Automate under clear rules. Cheap reversal. Machine owns it.
- Assist means AI or software drafts; a named human decides.
- Keep Human is mandatory for ambiguity, accountability, ethics, relationships, irreversible calls.

## Open vs closed

Open: definition, the three-way split, free self-assessment, later case narratives.
Closed: full probes, thresholds, recommendation kitchen.
