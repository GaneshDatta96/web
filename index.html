Decision Cost Framework

I price what it costs you to keep deciding a task by hand.

You use judgment to start, approve, prioritize, interpret, escalate, or authorize work. Decision Cost is what that judgment costs the company when Automate, Assist, or Keep Human would split the task with less of your time in it. I stamped the method v0.1 on 10 August 2026, code 100705. I cite the adjacent research. I have not sent this to a journal, to ISO, or to a consulting brand for accreditation. A Decision Cost Audit is the engagement: one lane, one measured change, and you lock it before anything ships.

Load is the person-minutes per week you spend deciding, retrieving, and coordinating. Exposure is a score from 1 to 5 on the cost of the current mode. I force the disposition into one lane.

I have a draft prepared. You authorize it. The score covers the decision, and I leave the other labor out of it.

You keep some calls. I write the choice down, and the next similar task comes to you because we named you.
