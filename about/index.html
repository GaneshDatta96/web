About

I'm Ganesh Datta.

My work has moved across marketing, creative direction, business operations and software. That range shapes how I approach projects: I care about the experience customers see, and I spend just as much time on the people who have to run it every day.

Today I direct brand and creative at Celeris. I've been marketing manager at Vande Wellness and marketing lead at VandeCart, and I build software: AyurDoc, clinical software for Ayurvedic practitioners, and THC CDSS, clinical decision support for Total Health Centers. I also host the Sapient Podcast.

I developed the Decision Cost Framework to find the recurring decisions that eat a team's time, and to sort them into what software should handle, where AI can assist and where a person should stay responsible.
