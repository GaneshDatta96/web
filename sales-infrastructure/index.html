Where it breaks

Four places the day leaks.

Booking

The call gets booked, then the follow-up waits on you. The buyer takes a meeting with whoever replied first. I put follow-up and no-show recovery on a rule.

CRM ownership

Every lead has a status. Not every lead has an owner. I assign ownership, define the next action, and make overdue leads visible.

Content

Posts go out when you have a free evening. I tie content to the conversations already in your pipeline and give publishing an owner who isn't you.

Admin

Reminders and status checks eat the hours you meant for sales calls. I move that work onto rules or to someone else.

Acceptance criteria

What works at handoff.

  • A test lead books, gets reminders, and receives the follow-up sequence without anyone touching it.
  • A no-show triggers the recovery message and a rebooking link.
  • Every open lead in the CRM has an owner and a next action, and overdue leads show up in one view.
  • You receive a written runbook: what was installed, which rules run, who owns each step, and how to change them.

We agree the scope and these checks before work starts. If something in the agreed scope doesn't pass at handoff, I fix it before the engagement closes. Revenue depends on your market, offer and selling, so I don't promise it.
